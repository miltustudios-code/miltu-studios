7. 7-Day Resume-to-Interview Sprint

A guided implementation layer gives the product an outcome instead of leaving the user with another template.

Day 1Build Career Vault: achievements, projects, skills, certifications.
Day 2Choose target roles and save 5 real job descriptions.
Day 3Create one base resume using the creator's recommended free tool stack.
Day 4Create 2 tailored variants from the same factual Career Vault.
Day 5Apply to best-fit roles and record each resume version used.
Day 6Follow up where appropriate and prepare a reusable interview story bank.
Day 7Review replies/interviews, identify the best-performing version, refine.
